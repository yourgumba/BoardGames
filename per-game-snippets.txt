PER-GAME SETUP
==============
In each game's HTML file, find the <head> section. If you pasted the earlier
snippet, REPLACE the four lines (manifest, theme-color, apple-mobile-web-app-capable,
apple-touch-icon) with the matching block below. Keep the <script> near </body>
that starts with 'if ("serviceWorker" in navigator)'. It stays the same.

--- ClueJuniorScorecard2.html ---
<link rel="manifest" href="manifest-clue-junior.json">
<meta name="theme-color" content="#b45309">
<meta name="apple-mobile-web-app-capable" content="yes">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="icon-clue-junior-192.png">
