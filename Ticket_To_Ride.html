Scoring cheat sheet

Route points: 1 train = 1, 2 = 2, 3 = 4, 4 = 7, 5 = 10, 6 = 15, 7 = 18, 8 = 21.
Completed tickets add their value; unfinished tickets subtract it.
Longest continuous route: +10 (ties: everyone tied gets it).
Europe: each unused station = +4. Final round starts when a player has 2 or fewer trains left.
